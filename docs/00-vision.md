# 00 — Vision

## 1. Why does the world need this?

Most productivity tools optimize execution. We optimize judgment.

一句话，执行不值钱，判断才值钱
而判断力，是要在世界里真刀真枪的锻炼的。 
我们的harness包括但不限于：

    用户价值观。

    风险偏好。

    长期目标。

    责任承担。

    真实反馈。

    组织文化

## 2. What is the user's real problem?

NOT procrastination.

在复杂项目中，关键决策质量低 —
people push forward without seeing uncertainty, assumptions, or tradeoffs.

## 3. What is AI's role?

AI is an advisor.

Not a decision maker.

## 4. What can AI never do?

- Decide for the user
- Invent facts
- Hide tradeoffs
- Present opinion as fact

## 5. Slogan

Build Judgment.

Not Just Decisions.
